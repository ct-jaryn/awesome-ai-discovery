# Awesome AI Discovery [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI tool directories, product databases, launch trackers, benchmarks, and learning resources — the places to go when you want to find, compare, and learn about AI tools.

The AI landscape ships hundreds of new tools every week. This list collects the discovery layers on top of it: directories that index tools, databases that structure product data, trackers that follow launches, leaderboards that compare models, and hubs that teach you how to use everything.

## AI Tool Directories

Browsable, categorized indexes of AI tools.

- [There's An AI For That](https://theresanaiforthat.com) - One of the largest AI tool aggregators, searchable by task.
- [Futurepedia](https://www.futurepedia.io) - Large AI tool directory with reviews and categories.
- [AI Seek Guide](https://aiseekguide.com) - Curated AI tools directory covering chat, agents, writing, image, video, coding, productivity, design, audio, and enterprise workflows.
- [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai) - AI tool directory ranked by traffic, updated daily.
- [Artificial Intelligence Directory](https://artificialintelligencedirectory.site) - Structured artificial intelligence product directory organized by use case.
- [Get AI Tools](https://getaitools.site) - AI tool recommendations and practical buying guides to help you choose the right tool.
- [TopAI.tools](https://topai.tools) - Search-first AI tool discovery engine.
- [Public Prompts](https://www.publicprompts.art/) - Categorized AI tools directory with searchable product profiles and official website links.

## AI Product Databases & Indexes

Structured, data-oriented views of the AI product landscape.

- [Global AI Product Index](https://globalaiproductindex.com) - A global database and market index for AI products: structured, citable profiles of AI companies and products.
- [Global AI Product](https://globalaiproduct.site) - Global AI product discovery and launch platform.
- [AIGC Tools Base](https://aigctoolsbase.online) - Independent AIGC tools database and resource platform.
- [Product Hunt AI](https://www.producthunt.com/topics/artificial-intelligence) - Community-voted AI product launches.
- [Crunchbase AI Companies](https://www.crunchbase.com/hub/artificial-intelligence-companies) - Funding and company data for the AI industry.
- [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai) - Indexed record of AI system failures and harms, useful for due diligence.

## Launch & Trend Trackers

Stay on top of what shipped this week.

- [AI Tools Daily](https://aitoolsdaily.site) - Daily AI tool launches, product updates, and industry trend coverage.
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) - Daily product launches, heavily weighted toward AI.
- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) - Where new AI tools and papers get discussed first.
- [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space) - Engineering-focused analysis of AI releases and trends.

## Benchmarks & Leaderboards

Compare models before you commit to one.

- [LMArena](https://lmarena.ai) - Crowdsourced head-to-head LLM rankings (formerly Chatbot Arena).
- [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai) - Independent benchmarks for quality, speed, and price across models and providers.
- [Open LLM Leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/open-llm-leaderboard/open_llm_leaderboard) - Standardized evaluation of open-weight models on Hugging Face.
- [SWE-bench](https://www.swebench.com) - Benchmark for AI coding agents on real GitHub issues.

## Learning Resources & Guides

Learn how to actually use the tools: guides, APIs, prompts, datasets.

- [AI Resource Hub](https://airesourcehub.site) - Knowledge infrastructure for AI developers: guides, APIs, datasets, prompts, and open-source projects in one searchable learning space.
- [Learn Prompting](https://learnprompting.org) - Free, open-source prompt engineering course.
- [Prompt Engineering Guide](https://www.promptingguide.ai) - Comprehensive prompting techniques with papers and examples.
- [DeepLearning.AI](https://www.deeplearning.ai) - Short courses on LLMs, agents, and applied AI.
- [fast.ai](https://www.fast.ai) - Practical deep learning courses for coders.
- [OpenAI Cookbook](https://cookbook.openai.com) - Official examples and guides for the OpenAI API.
- [Anthropic Cookbook](https://github.com/anthropics/anthropic-cookbook) - Notebooks and recipes for building with Claude.
- [Generative AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners) - Microsoft's free 21-lesson course on building with generative AI.
- [Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn) - Free courses on NLP, deep RL, audio, and agents.

## Model & Research Hubs

Where models and papers live.

- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co) - Models, datasets, spaces, and the de facto open-source AI hub.
- [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com) - Research papers matched with code and benchmarks.
- [arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent) - Preprints for AI research.
- [Ollama Library](https://ollama.com/library) - Runnable local LLMs, one command away.
- [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) - Unified API and pricing overview for hundreds of hosted models.
- [ModelScope](https://modelscope.cn) - Alibaba's open model hub, strong on Chinese-language models.
